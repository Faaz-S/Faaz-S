## 💼 Experience

### Hydro One

**Electrical Engineering Support Co-op**

Worked alongside Network Management teams supporting real-time electricity grid operations across Ontario.

* 💻 Developed and maintained internal applications used department-wide, helping automate tedious manual processes and streamline day-to-day workflows**
* 🔌 Gained hands-on exposure to transmission and distribution systems, including feeders, transformers, breakers, protection schemes, fault analysis, and transfer trips**
* 🚨 Worked with outage management processes and saw how planning teams, control centres, and field crews coordinate during planned and emergency work**
* 📡 Gained exposure to the telecom infrastructure supporting the electrical grid, including fibre optic and SONET communication systems**
* 🤝 Learned how Hydro One coordinates with organizations such as IESO and OPG to maintain reliable power system operations**

Working at Hydro One gave me a different perspective on software, especially seeing how the tools people build can directly support teams working with critical infrastructure.
